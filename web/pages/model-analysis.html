Model Analysis

ET — expected time

ET is a descriptive figure, not a model input. It carries real standalone signal (AUC 0.658 predicting the next run) but adds nothing on top of the market price once log-odds are controlled (p = 0.746). Read it; do not build selection rules on it.

NoHorsePl TimeFigure vs par vs race neffConfidenceLevel

vs par and vs race answer different questions and are shown separately — the v4 model conflated them. A par time is a property of the race: every runner above shares one, and if that count is ever not 1 the model is wrong regardless of its accuracy.
